# Room Memory

A LAN party game for 1–10 players on the same Wi-Fi. Everybody spawns in the
same room and gets **1:30** to look at it. Then the screen goes black, and
five questions ask what was actually in there.

game handed round between friends.

## How to play

1. One of you presses **Create Room**. Everybody else presses **Join Room** and
   picks it from the list — you all need to be on the same Wi-Fi.
2. The host presses **Play**, and everybody votes for which room to play.
3. **1:30 to look around.** Walk about and remember as much as you can.
4. **Close your eyes.** The room goes.
5. **Five questions, 15 seconds each.** *How many chairs were in the room?
   What colour was the rug? What was standing on the desk?* Four options, one
   answer, and your first tap is final. Every question runs its full fifteen
   seconds, so a round takes the same time however many of you are playing —
   and a question you do not answer scores nothing.
6. **Results.** Who remembered the most, and gems for what you got right.
